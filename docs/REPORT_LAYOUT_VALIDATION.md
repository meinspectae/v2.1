## Phase A — engine and photos

### Changed

- A4 portrait with 15 mm outside margins; header/footer use their existing content.
- Replaced report page screenshots with vector text and independent image objects. Inter regular/bold/italic and Noto Naskh Arabic are embedded; Arabic is shaped right-to-left and its PDF Unicode mapping supports searching.
- Fresh evidence capture contains the complete oriented frame in 900 × 1200 or 1200 × 900 pixels, JPEG quality 0.80 and explicit 4:2:0 subsampling. The live cover capture allows 1200 × 1600 (or landscape equivalent). Camera fallback may use the standard resolution, which is within the cover maximum.
- Original captured JPEG bytes are embedded without a second resize in the normal export path. Existing larger JPEG evidence also retains its original dimensions unless the size limit is exceeded.
- Photo boxes are 50 × 66 mm portrait or 79 × 59 mm landscape, with contained images, light-grey borders/rounded frames, 3 mm horizontal gaps and centred 8 pt timestamps. Full image corners remain visible rather than being clipped by rounding.
- A row is kept together. Tall multi-row items receive continuation cards rather than sliced photos.
- The existing 40 MiB sharing ceiling is retained. Oversize export retries lower quality by 0.05 per pass before any dimension reduction. No new user step or validation was added.

### Tested

- Phase A fixture: 32 independent JPEG photos, 8 pages, 2,018,377 bytes (2.02 MB / 1.93 MiB).
- Final fixture: 34 independent JPEG photos, all 900 × 1200 or 1200 × 900, explicit 4:2:0, with original corner markers retained.
- A two-pixel synthetic surface mark remains visible in a 300% PDF crop.
- Embedded fonts, text extraction/search, photo aspect ratios and page boundaries checked against the generated PDF.

### How to review

Open the Phase A PDF in a PDF reader, set zoom to 300%, and inspect the fine dark mark near the centre of a wall photo. Check all blue corner markers, select/copy a sentence and search for “scratch” and “العقار”. Confirm the download is below 20 MB. Repeat with a real camera photo of a small mark for device acceptance; the synthetic check does not prove every phone camera's sharpness.

## Scope and remaining acceptance checks

- Rating calculations, checklist normalization/content, signature capture, report hash algorithm, storage, authentication, payments and backend behavior were not changed by this report update.
- No new mandatory workflow step, blocking validation or application screen was added. The existing finalisation confirmation and sharing limit remain.
- Existing signature records do not contain a separately captured per-signer IP. The report uses a signer IP if already present; otherwise it honestly prints “IP: Not recorded” rather than substituting the inspector's IP. Adding that capture would exceed the requested scope.
- Synthetic fixture QR/link is displayed but not registered as a real inspection. It is not an end-to-end verification success test.
- Real-device EXIF/camera acceptance, authenticated finalisation/email delivery and production publication were not exercised in this task.
- The current normal-sized fixtures do not exercise the entire over-40-MiB quality/dimension retry ladder; that order was reviewed in the renderer. Under-20-MB acceptance is confirmed for both supplied synthetic fixtures, not promised for every possible inspection.
